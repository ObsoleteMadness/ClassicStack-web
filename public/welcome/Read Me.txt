Welcome to Browser Share

This volume lives in this web browser. When a TashTalk adaptor is connected, ClassicStack shares it over LocalTalk AFP as “Browser Share” (server name “ClassicStack”).

• Drag files here from this computer, or copy them from a Macintosh that has mounted the share.
• Resource forks are preserved (AppleSingle / AppleDouble).
• The Utilities folder is a home for starter tools such as archive expanders and disk utilities.

To restore these starter files later, right-click Browser Share in the sidebar and choose “Add Welcome Pack Items”. Items you have changed or replaced are left alone.
